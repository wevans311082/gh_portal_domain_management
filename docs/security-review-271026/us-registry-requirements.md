# US registry declarations and admission

The reviewed .US contact policy uses general Contact records with all four contact roles and prohibits privacy/proxy purchases. No .US price or advertised extension is automatically created; an offering still needs explicit configuration. Current automated nexus categories cover individual citizens/permanent residents and organizations constituted under US law. Foreign-entity categories remain a documented review path while the registry country-declaration contract is unresolved; they are not counted as a complete automated adapter.

Saved contacts have optional nexus category, intended-use and certification fields. Existing contacts acquire blank declarations, not inferred citizenship. Address country is never used to invent nexus: a US citizen can have a foreign address, and a US address alone does not establish citizenship. Individuals need no company number or Companies House check. Forms require complete, compatible declarations and explicit certification; editing a saved declaration requires re-certification. Local declarations are not external eligibility approval.

Only the registrant payload contains purpose/category attributes. Administrative/technical/billing contacts use the general contact payload without those requirements. Immutable bindings and preparation caches distinguish these payload versions, including when one saved contact fills all roles. Registration/transfer preparation and contact replacement validate every owned role before mutation, then recheck version digests and binding IDs. No shared remote contact is modified.

Registration validates standard 3-63 character ASCII labels and 1-10 year terms; shorter reserved/premium names and unsupported IDNs need review. Live availability/registry acceptance remains authoritative. Privacy is disabled in the registration form, suppressed in cart materialization and constrained by the provider payload. The customer sees the nexus and public-data requirements. The generic organization-publication opt-in attribute is not applied to this country-code registry. .US is not labelled as the generic ICANN RAA email workflow and gets no inferred RAA deadline/resend.

US transfers require the documented 6-16 character authorization code. Invalid shape stops before invoice/provider admission, and codes are never included in validation messages. Broader transfer progress and encrypted auth-code storage remain roadmap item 7; this increment does not close that workstream.

Renewal requires a consistent captured registration/transfer association and current nexus declaration. The latest matching confirmed contact-change digest supersedes the original paid snapshot without rewriting it. Missing or edited declarations require review before renewal invoice/provider work, including automatic renewal. The generic renewal-review UI explains the hold and permits auto-renew opt-out.

Staff may select a saved contact or supply a complete replacement with explicit declaration. Staff overrides create a new contact; blank fields are not filled with invented values. Customer choices, staff fields, cart checkout and paid workers share the same underlying validation. The code does not claim a provider eligibility-check success from undocumented status tokens.
