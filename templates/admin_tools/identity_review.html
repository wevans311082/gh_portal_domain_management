Scope of this review

Individuals can receive a local policy review without a business or company number. Organisations, overseas businesses and sole traders can receive a local business review without a UK company number. Choose the assessment actually completed; each outcome remains independent. This records an owner's assessment under the referenced policy. It does not verify registrar email, TLD eligibility, citizenship or registry approval, and it is not required merely to save accurate contact details.

Collect no identity documents or identity numbers through this screen. Before requesting evidence elsewhere, your approved policy must define the need, authorized access and retention. Record only its policy version and review case reference here.
